# Loyalty Program (FetchPoints)

**Category:** Loyalty

FetchPoints is Fetchly's free loyalty program. You're automatically enrolled when you create an account — there's nothing separate to sign up for.

**Earning points:** 1 point per $1 spent on orders, plus bonus points for actions like leaving a product review (25 points), referring a friend (100 points), or your pet's "birthday month" (50 points, set in your profile).

**Redeeming points:** Points can be redeemed at checkout in increments of 500 points = $5 off. Points do not expire as long as your account has at least one order every 12 months.

**Tiers:** Spending $150+ in a calendar year unlocks Fetchly Gold, which adds free expedited shipping and early access to sales. Tier status is recalculated each January based on the prior year's spend.

Points are not transferable between accounts and have no cash value outside of Fetchly purchases.
