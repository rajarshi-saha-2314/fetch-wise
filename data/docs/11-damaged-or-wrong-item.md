# Damaged, Defective, or Wrong Item

**Category:** Order Issues

If your order arrives damaged, defective, or different from what you ordered, Fetchly will send a free replacement or a full refund — your choice — with no return of the incorrect item required in most cases.

To report the issue, go to Order History, select the order, and choose "Report a Problem." Please include a photo of the item and, if applicable, the packaging. Reports must be submitted within **14 days of delivery**.

Replacements ship free via standard shipping once the report is reviewed, which usually happens within 1 business day. If the item is out of stock, we'll issue a refund instead.

This process is specifically for items that arrived wrong or damaged — for items you simply want to send back, see our Returns & Refunds Policy instead.
