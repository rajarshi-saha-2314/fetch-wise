# Shipping Policy

**Category:** Shipping

Fetchly ships all orders from our fulfillment centers in Ohio and Nevada. Standard shipping within the contiguous United States takes **3-5 business days** after the order is processed. Orders placed before 1:00 PM ET usually leave the warehouse the same day; orders placed after that ship the next business day.

We offer three shipping speeds at checkout:

- **Standard (3-5 business days):** Free on orders over $35, otherwise $4.99.
- **Expedited (2 business days):** $9.99 flat rate.
- **Overnight (1 business day):** $19.99 flat rate, only available for orders placed before 12:00 PM ET.

Shipping times are estimates and begin once an order has been processed, not when it was placed. Fetchly does not ship on weekends or federal holidays. Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories may see an additional 2-3 days of transit time.

For questions about a specific order's shipping status, use order tracking rather than this general policy.
