# International Shipping

**Category:** Shipping

Fetchly currently ships to the United States (including Alaska, Hawaii, and U.S. territories) and Canada. We do not ship outside North America at this time.

**Canada shipping:** Orders take 7-10 business days and are subject to Canadian customs duties and taxes, which are the customer's responsibility and are not included in the Fetchly checkout price. Canadian orders ship from our Nevada warehouse only, so product availability may differ slightly from U.S. listings.

**Unsupported regions:** If you enter a shipping address outside the U.S. or Canada at checkout, you'll see an error and the order will not be able to be placed. We don't offer freight-forwarding partnerships or workarounds for unsupported countries.

We're often asked when international shipping outside North America will expand — we don't have a public timeline to share for that.
