# Contact Us & Support Hours

**Category:** Support

Fetchly's support team is available:

- **Chat & email:** Monday-Friday, 8:00 AM - 8:00 PM ET, and Saturday-Sunday, 9:00 AM - 5:00 PM ET.
- **Phone:** Monday-Friday, 9:00 AM - 6:00 PM ET, at 1-800-FETCHLY.

Email response times are typically under 4 hours during business hours and by the next business day outside those hours. Chat is generally the fastest way to reach us for order issues.

Support is closed on New Year's Day, Memorial Day, Independence Day, Labor Day, Thanksgiving, and Christmas Day.

For fastest service, have your order ID (format FCH-XXXXX) ready before reaching out — most issues can be resolved without escalation if we can look up the order directly.
