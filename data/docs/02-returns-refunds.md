# Returns & Refunds Policy

**Category:** Returns

Most unopened, unused Fetchly products can be returned within **30 days of delivery** for a full refund to the original payment method. Opened bags of food or treats can still be returned within 30 days if your pet had an adverse reaction — we just ask you to note this in the return request so our team can flag it for quality review.

To start a return, log into your Fetchly account, go to Order History, and select "Start a Return" on the relevant order. We'll email a prepaid return label within one business day.

Refunds are issued once the returned item is scanned by the carrier (not once it arrives at our warehouse), and typically appear on your statement within **5-7 business days**. Original shipping fees are non-refundable unless the return is due to a Fetchly error (wrong item shipped, damaged in transit, etc.).

Clearance items and gift cards are final sale and cannot be returned or refunded.
