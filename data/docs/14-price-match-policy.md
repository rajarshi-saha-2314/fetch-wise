# Price Match Policy

**Category:** Pricing

Fetchly offers a limited price match for orders that have not yet shipped. If the exact same product (same brand, size, and variant) drops in price on fetchly.com within **7 days** of your purchase, we'll refund the difference as store credit — contact support with your order ID to request this.

Fetchly does not price match against other retailers, third-party marketplace sellers, or limited-time flash sales (typically 24 hours or less). Subscription discounts, FetchPoints redemptions, and promo codes are not eligible for price matching, since these are account-specific rather than list-price changes.

Price match requests must be submitted within the 7-day window; we're unable to make exceptions for requests submitted after prices have changed again.
