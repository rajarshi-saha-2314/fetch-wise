# Gift Cards

**Category:** Gift Cards

Fetchly gift cards are sold in denominations of $25, $50, $100, and $150, and are delivered by email (digital only — we do not sell physical gift cards). Delivery is instant unless you schedule it for a future date, such as a holiday.

Gift cards never expire and carry no maintenance fees. They can be applied at checkout in the same field as promo codes, and a single order can use multiple gift cards along with one promo code.

Gift card balances can be checked at fetchly.com/gift-cards/balance using the card code from the email. Gift cards cannot be redeemed for cash, reloaded, or transferred to a different email address once sent, and lost gift card codes cannot be recovered unless the original purchase order ID is provided to support.

As noted in our Returns Policy, gift cards themselves are final sale and non-refundable once purchased.
