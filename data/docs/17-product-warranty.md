# Product Warranty

**Category:** Warranty

Hard goods sold by Fetchly (bowls, feeders, carriers, beds, toys with electronic components, etc.) carry a **1-year manufacturer's warranty** against defects in materials or workmanship from the date of delivery. This covers things like a feeder motor failing or stitching failing on a carrier under normal use.

The warranty does not cover normal wear and tear, damage from chewing or misuse, or damage from washing items not labeled machine-washable. Consumable items (food, treats, litter, waste bags) are not covered by warranty — see our Returns Policy and Product Safety & Recalls policy for those instead.

To file a warranty claim, contact support with your order ID, a description of the issue, and a photo. Approved claims result in a free replacement; if the item is discontinued, we'll offer a comparable product or store credit.

Warranty claims do not require the original packaging, but do require proof of purchase through your Fetchly order history.
