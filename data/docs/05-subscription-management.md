# Subscription Management

**Category:** Subscriptions

Fetchly's subscription (the "Fetch Box") lets you auto-ship food, treats, or supplies on a recurring schedule — every 2, 4, 6, or 8 weeks. You can manage your subscription anytime from Account > Subscriptions.

**Skipping a delivery:** Select "Skip Next Delivery" at least 3 days before the scheduled ship date. Skipped deliveries do not charge your card.

**Changing frequency or products:** You can swap products or change delivery frequency at any time; changes apply to the next unshipped order.

**Pausing a subscription:** Pausing stops all future shipments and charges until you manually resume it. Paused subscriptions do not expire.

**Billing:** Your card is charged 1 day before each shipment, not on a fixed calendar date, so exact charge dates shift if you skip or reschedule.

To cancel a subscription entirely rather than pause it, see our Cancellation Policy.
