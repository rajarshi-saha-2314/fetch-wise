# Order Tracking & Order Status

**Category:** Orders

Every Fetchly order gets an order ID in the format **FCH-XXXXX** (five digits), shown on your confirmation email and in Order History. Once an order ships, you'll receive a shipping confirmation email with a tracking link.

Order statuses you may see:

- **Processing:** We've received your order and are preparing it for shipment.
- **Shipped:** The order has left our warehouse and is with the carrier.
- **Out for Delivery:** The carrier has it on a truck for delivery that day.
- **Delivered:** The carrier has marked the package as delivered.
- **Delayed:** The carrier has reported an unexpected delay (weather, mechanical issue, etc.).

If you need to check the live status of a specific order, provide your order ID and we can look up the current status, carrier, and expected delivery date directly rather than relying on general shipping estimates.

Note that tracking information can take up to 24 hours to update after a status change.
