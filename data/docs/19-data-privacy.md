# Data Privacy & Account Data

**Category:** Privacy

Fetchly collects the information needed to fulfill orders and run your account: name, shipping/billing address, email, phone number, order history, and pet profile details (name, breed, birthday) you choose to add. We do not sell customer data to third parties.

Payment card details are handled entirely by our PCI-compliant payment processor — Fetchly's own systems never store full card numbers.

**Your data rights:** You can request a copy of your personal data or request full deletion of your account and associated data by contacting support. Deletion requests are processed within 30 days and are irreversible.

**Marketing communications:** You can unsubscribe from marketing emails at any time via the link at the bottom of any marketing email, or from Account Settings > Communication Preferences. Transactional emails (order confirmations, shipping updates, password resets) cannot be turned off, since they're required to service your account.

For our full privacy policy and details on cookies and analytics, see fetchly.com/privacy.
