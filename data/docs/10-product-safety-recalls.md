# Product Safety & Recalls

**Category:** Product Safety

Fetchly monitors all manufacturer recall notices for products we sell. If a product you've purchased is recalled, we will email every affected customer directly with instructions — you do not need to monitor this yourself, but you can also check fetchly.com/recalls at any time for the current list.

If your pet shows signs of illness you believe may be related to food or treats (vomiting, lethargy, loss of appetite), stop feeding the product immediately and contact your veterinarian first. After that, you can report the issue to Fetchly through Order History > Report a Problem so we can log it and, if needed, escalate to the manufacturer.

Recalled products can be returned for a full refund regardless of how much has been used or how long ago it was purchased — the normal 30-day return window does not apply to recalls.

Fetchly does not provide veterinary advice; our support team can only help with order- and product-related questions.
