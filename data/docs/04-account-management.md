# Account Management

**Category:** Account

You can manage your Fetchly account at fetchly.com/account. Common account tasks:

**Resetting your password:** Click "Forgot Password" on the login page and enter the email associated with your account. You'll receive a reset link valid for 60 minutes. If you don't see the email, check your spam folder before requesting a new one.

**Updating your email address:** Go to Account Settings > Profile. You'll need to confirm the change via a verification email sent to the new address.

**Updating payment methods:** Go to Account Settings > Payment Methods to add, remove, or set a default card. Removing a card that's tied to an active subscription will pause that subscription until a new payment method is added.

**Updating shipping addresses:** Go to Account Settings > Addresses. Changing your default address does not affect orders that have already been placed.

**Deleting your account:** Contact support to request account deletion. This is permanent and cancels any active subscriptions.
