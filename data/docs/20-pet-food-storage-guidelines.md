# Pet Food Storage & Handling Guidelines

**Category:** Product Info

To keep Fetchly food and treats fresh, store dry food in its original bag (the packaging is designed to block light and moisture) inside an airtight container, in a cool, dry place away from direct sunlight. Once opened, dry food is best used within **6 weeks** for full freshness, even though it remains safe to feed past that window if stored properly.

Refrigerate opened wet food and use within 3 days. Do not freeze wet food, as this can affect texture. Unopened wet food and treats should be stored at room temperature and used by the best-by date printed on the package.

Do not store any pet food in direct sunlight, near an oven, or in a garage subject to large temperature swings, as heat accelerates spoilage.

This guidance is general care information, not veterinary advice — if you believe a specific product made your pet sick, see our Product Safety & Recalls policy and consult your veterinarian.
