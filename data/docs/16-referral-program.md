# Referral Program

**Category:** Promotions

Every Fetchly account has a unique referral link, found under Account > Refer a Friend. When someone places their first order using your link, they get **20% off** that order and you receive **$10 in FetchPoints credit** once their order ships (not when they place it, to prevent abuse from cancelled orders).

There's no limit to how many friends you can refer or how much referral credit you can earn. Referral credit is added automatically — no action needed on your part.

Referral links only apply to new customers; if the referred person already has a Fetchly account, the link will not apply the discount. Referral credit cannot be combined with a promo code on the same order.

Referral credit follows the same expiration rule as standard FetchPoints (see our Loyalty Program policy) — it doesn't expire as long as your account has at least one order every 12 months.
