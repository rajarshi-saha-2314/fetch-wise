# Payment Methods & Billing

**Category:** Billing

Fetchly accepts Visa, Mastercard, American Express, Discover, PayPal, and Apple Pay. We do not accept checks, money orders, or cryptocurrency.

Your card is only charged once an order ships, not when it's placed — the exception is subscription orders, which are charged 1 day before shipment (see Subscription Management).

**Failed payments:** If a payment fails, we'll retry automatically after 24 hours and again after 72 hours. After two failed attempts, the order (or subscription shipment) is cancelled and you'll receive an email to update your payment method.

**Billing descriptor:** Charges appear on your statement as "FETCHLY INC." If you don't recognize a charge with this descriptor, check Order History first — most cases are a subscription renewal.

**Sales tax:** Tax is calculated based on your shipping address and shown at checkout before you confirm your order.
